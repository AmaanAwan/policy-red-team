System Scope & Purpose

✅ What it IS: An adversarial AI stress-testing platform (ADK Attacker vs. Defender vs. Judge) that audits Pakistani statutory policies and private sector compliance frameworks to discover regulatory loopholes, jurisdictional arbitrage, and enforcement gaps.

❌ What it IS NOT: Not a line-by-line compliance checklist tool, not a legal advisory bot for individuals, not for non-statutory documents (like visions/manifestos), and not a case-law / judicial precedent search engine.

📖 Getting Started — Testing Guidelines & Document Suitability
ℹ️
How to use this platform: The system deploys an adversarial AttackerAgent to exploit ambiguous language, while a DefenderAgent cites qualifying clauses and superior statutes. A senior JudgeAgent evaluates the debate under Pakistan statutory law.
✅ Optimal Documents to Audit
  • Subordinate Regulations & Bylaws: Building codes, municipal traffic regulations, zoning rules.
  • Statutory S.R.O.s & Notifications: Tax exemptions, customs schedules, tariff directives.
  • Compliance Policies: Clear operational duties, quantitative thresholds, timelines, or penalty provisions.
  • Parent Acts (as Supporting doc): The superior Act granting statutory rule-making power.
❌ Incompatible / Inefficient Documents
  • High-Level Policy Visions: Aspirational strategy papers (e.g. "Vision 2030") with no binding legal clauses.
  • Judicial Case Law / Court Judgements: Court rulings require jurisprudence synthesis; this tool audits statutory texts.
  • Un-OCRed Scanned PDFs: Scanned photocopies without readable text cannot be parsed.
  • Non-Pakistani Statutes: Foreign laws lack local statutory grounding and produce lower confidence verdicts.
Quick Workflow: 1. Upload the target regulation in 🎯 Target Policy  ·  2. Upload governing Act in 🛡️ Parent Statute (optional)  ·  3. Click Run Red Team Analysis (3–5 min debate).
